# Regional SEO rollout

## Scope shipped

- Header language control replaces the redundant Search navigation link; the search input remains. Eight language versions: English, Portuguese, Spanish, Indonesian, Turkish, Vietnamese, Polish, Arabic.
- Native details/links support keyboard navigation, Escape, disabled-JavaScript language navigation, locale cookies through next-intl, and query/hash preservation when opening the menu. English-only guides fall back to a translated homepage when no equivalent exists.
- Spanish, Polish, Arabic: translated interface and metadata, individual canonical URLs, reciprocal hreflang, root sitemaps, Arabic RTL.
- Puzzle category editorial text localized across all eight languages. Arrow Exit Puzzle, Arrow Cube 3D and 2048 Knights localized in Portuguese, Spanish, Polish and Arabic.
- New-language game/category pages without substantive editorial translations remain accessible but noindex, excluded from sitemaps and from equivalent-language annotations. This avoids submitting thousands of English-content copies as a new SEO campaign.

Arabic serves more than one country, and no assumption is made that every visitor in the Gulf searches in Arabic. These seeds are editorial hypotheses, not Trends-verified local rankings.

## How to select winners

1. Trends: compare terms within each country over 90 days and 12 months; check relevant Rising/Top queries. Relative interest is not absolute volume or SEO competition.
2. Search results: manually assess the pages ranking for a local query, whether they meet the play intent, quality of explanations, and whether a genuinely better game experience can be provided. Ranking opportunity cannot be inferred from country alone.
3. GSC: query × landing page × country × device. Compare 28-day windows once available; prioritize relevant queries with impressions around positions 8–20. CTR comparisons need similar rank/device/country.
4. Revenue: aggregate Adsterra + Playgama attributed publisher revenue per country and divide by site sessions to compute net session RPM. Keep ad-impression CPM separate. Apply the same dates/timezones and allow for provider reporting lag. Revenue does not prove return visits or gameplay quality.
5. Initial operating gate: 1,000 real sessions per candidate country over multiple dates is a directional pilot threshold, not a statistical guarantee. Low traffic means wait or group reporting for exploration, not declare a high-paying country from a few impressions.
6. Prioritize the intersection of attainable search traffic, game engagement, and measured net revenue. Small high-RPM cohorts alone may not yield the most total income.

No GSC, country revenue export, or country-specific Trends export was accessed for this change. No verified CPM forecast or ranking guarantee is available.
