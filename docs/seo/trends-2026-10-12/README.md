# First Trends-led SEO batch

Source: four user-exported Google Trends related-query CSVs; worldwide, October 11–12, 2026, 24-hour window as encoded in the filenames. The exports do not contain the original seed terms or category filters. Puzzle and Gomoku are inferred directions from the query rows, not a reconstruction of all selected filters.

Interest is relative within each export, not monthly volume. Do not compare the two clusters' scores as absolute demand. Breakout can reflect a small prior base. One day of data cannot establish a lasting trend or ranking opportunity. Exclude unrelated queries (e.g. flights), competitor navigation (Poki, Friv, Cool Math Games), and unavailable named games.

Rules references: https://www.renju.net/gomokurules/ and https://gomoku.renju.net/rules/ . Standard and freestyle differ on overlines; app-specific openings may differ. Renju restrictions are not presented as universal Gomoku rules.

## Follow-up measurement

Search Console data was not accessed during this change. Start with query × page × country × device, monitor 7-day signals and use 28-day comparisons when available. Do not assume these worldwide English queries describe the site's eventual audience.

- Confirm English guide and revised puzzle page are indexable; submit the existing English sitemap if not already submitted.
- Once impressions appear, prioritize relevant queries around positions 8–20; improve page substance and internal links.
- Evaluate CTR among similar positions, countries and devices; do not impose a universal CTR threshold.
- Track organic landing sessions and verified gameplay events alongside clicks. A game page view is not proof that gameplay began, and GSC does not provide per-user query attribution.
- After 28 days, retain clusters showing relevant search growth and player engagement. Revisit long-window Trends before extending this batch.

No invented search volumes, growth guarantees, playable Gomoku claim, or FAQ-rich-result promise.
